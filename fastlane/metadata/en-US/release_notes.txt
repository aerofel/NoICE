No-ICE 2.1 adds a device integrity check.

DEVICE INTEGRITY CHECK
- At launch, No-ICE checks that your iPhone or iPad has not been jailbroken
- On a jailbroken device the app shows a clear notice instead of hold-over times, because the integrity of the app and its data can no longer be guaranteed there
- The check runs entirely on the device: no network, no account, nothing is sent anywhere
- Standard iPhones and iPads are unaffected, and No-ICE on Mac works exactly as before

Always cross-check against the current official FAA or Transport Canada tables and your operator's procedures.
