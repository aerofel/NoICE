No-ICE 2.0 is a major update: a redesigned app and a new way to work as a crew.

NEARBY SHARE
- A new button under the settings gear sends your running timer, weather condition, temperature, fluid, mix, flaps and FAA/TCA source to every nearby iPhone or iPad with No-ICE open
- No pairing and no network needed: it uses Bluetooth and works on the ramp
- The button lights up with a device count when someone is in range
- The receiving crew member sees a summary and confirms before anything changes, with a warning if it would replace their own running timer
- Only devices carrying the same FAA and Transport Canada table season can share

REDESIGNED FLUID PICKER
- Fluid types as colour-coded pills with the count of approved products
- Products listed by brand and name, with search and your three most recent fluids
- Mix chips (100/0, 75/25, 50/50) showing assured and limit times, one tap to switch
- A hero card for the selected fluid that mirrors the Live Activity

REDESIGNED WEATHER AND TIMER
- Temperature steppers, precipitation and intensity filter chips, and a clear hero row for the selected condition
- Timer card with Zulu time pills, a context strip, and hold-over durations shown before start
- Flaps/slats as two clear cards, available as soon as a condition is selected
- Header status line showing source, season and unit, one tap to change them

LIVE ACTIVITY
- Lock screen layout rebuilt on two rows so condition, temperature, fluid, flaps and paused state are always readable
- Dynamic Island uses the fluid colours

FIXES
- A fluid dropped because the temperature left its band no longer reappears after relaunch
- Text scales instead of wrapping at large Dynamic Type sizes

Always cross-check against the current official FAA or Transport Canada tables and your operator's procedures.
