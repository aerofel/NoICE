No-ICE is the streamlined hold-over time companion built for professional pilots. Get instant access to official FAA and Transport Canada de-icing tables, track your hold-over times with precision, share them with your crew, and stay informed from cockpit to lock screen.

Whether you're operating in light freezing drizzle or heavy snow, No-ICE filters through complex hold-over time tables in seconds, so you can focus on what matters: a safe departure.

OFFICIAL DATA, ALWAYS CURRENT
- FAA and Transport Canada (TCA) hold-over time tables, Winter 2026-27 edition
- Type I, II, III, and IV fluid coverage
- All weather conditions: snow, freezing rain, freezing drizzle, frost, and more
- Multiple fluid concentrations and temperature ranges

FAST FLUID SELECTION
- Fluid types as colour-coded pills with the number of approved products
- Search products by brand and name, with your three most recent fluids at the top
- Mix chips (100/0, 75/25, 50/50) showing assured and limit times at a glance
- The selected fluid in a clear hero card

PRECISION TIMER
- One-tap start, hold, and resume with animated visual feedback
- Elapsed time and UTC/Zulu times displayed at a glance
- Assured time (green) and Limit time (orange) calculated in real time
- Automatic flaps/slats factor (0.76 reduction) when extended
- Hold marks to log each check during multi-step procedures

NEARBY SHARE
- Send the running timer, weather, temperature, fluid, mix and flaps to nearby iPhones and iPads with one tap
- Bluetooth only: no pairing, no network, works on the ramp
- The receiver reviews a summary and confirms before anything changes
- Only devices with the same table season can share

LIVE ACTIVITY & DYNAMIC ISLAND
- Monitor your hold-over countdown directly from the lock screen
- Dynamic Island integration for at-a-glance status
- Visual progress bar with color-coded assured and limit zones
- Bar turns fully red when hold-over time is exceeded
- All key data visible without opening the app

BUILT FOR THE FLIGHT DECK
- Celsius and Fahrenheit support
- Smart weather filtering by precipitation type and intensity
- Persistent state: resume exactly where you left off after a restart
- Haptic feedback for confident, eyes-free operation
- Device integrity check: refuses to run on a jailbroken iPhone or iPad, entirely offline
- Optimized for iPhone and iPad
